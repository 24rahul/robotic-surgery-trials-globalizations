## Key Findings

| Metric | 2001 | 2025 | Change |
|--------|------|------|--------|
| Annual Trials | 26 | 292 | ↑ 11× |
| Active Countries | 11 | 24 | ↑ 2.2× |
| Multi-Country Share | 3.8% | 26.4% | ↑ 7× |
| HHI (Concentration) | 0.182 | 0.121 | ↓ 33% |
| North America Share | 34.6% | 19.5% | ↓ 44% |
| Europe Share | 38.5% | 55.5% | ↑ 44% |
| Asia Share | 26.9% | 52.7% | ↑ 96% |
