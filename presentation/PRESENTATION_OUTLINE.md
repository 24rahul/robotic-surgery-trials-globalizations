# 8-Minute Oral Presentation Outline

## Globalization of Robotic Surgery Clinical Trials: A 25-Year Bibliometric Analysis

**Authors:** K. Madapati, R. Gorijavolu, I. Shoyombo, T. Ademola, L.A. Celi

**Time:** 8 minutes (aim for ~7:30 to allow for transitions)

---

## Slide-by-Slide Structure

### SLIDE 1: Title Slide (30 seconds)
**Content:**
- Title: "Globalization of Robotic Surgery Clinical Trials: A 25-Year Bibliometric Analysis"
- Authors and affiliations
- Conference name/date

**Speaking points:**
- Brief greeting
- "Today I'll present our analysis of how robotic surgery clinical trials have expanded and globalized over the past 25 years"

---

### SLIDE 2: Background & Motivation (45 seconds)
**Content:**
- Robotic surgery adoption has grown dramatically since the da Vinci system (FDA approved 2000)
- Clinical trial evidence is crucial for validating safety and efficacy
- Gap: Geographic distribution and globalization patterns remain undefined

**Speaking points:**
- "Robotic surgery has transformed surgical practice across multiple specialties"
- "However, the geographic scope and patterns of clinical trial activity have not been systematically characterized"
- "We set out to quantify the globalization of robotic surgery clinical trials from 2001 to 2025"

---

### SLIDE 3: Methods (45 seconds)
**Figure:** Flow diagram or bullet points

**Content:**
- Data source: PubMed/MEDLINE 2025 Baseline
- Filtering pipeline:
  1. Clinical trials (PublicationType)
  2. Surgery-related (87 keywords)
  3. Robotic surgery (MeSH + keywords)
  4. True clinical trials (RCT or Clinical Trial)
- Metrics: HHI, multi-country share, regional distributions
- Country attribution: Author affiliations (fallback: journal country)

**Speaking points:**
- "We processed PubMed baseline data through a multi-stage filtering pipeline"
- "Final dataset included only RCTs and clinical trials"
- "We calculated globalization metrics including the Herfindahl-Hirschman Index"

---

### SLIDE 4: Trial Volume Growth (1 minute)
**Figure:** `slide_trial_growth.png`

**Key numbers to emphasize:**
- 2001: 26 trials
- 2025: 292 trials  
- **11-fold increase**

**Speaking points:**
- "Annual clinical trials increased 11-fold over 25 years"
- "Note the acceleration after 2020, with 292 trials published in 2025 alone"
- "This reflects the maturation of robotic surgery as a field requiring rigorous evaluation"

---

### SLIDE 5: Globalization Metrics (1 minute)
**Figure:** `slide_globalization_metrics.png`

**Key numbers:**
- HHI: 0.18 → 0.09 (50% decrease in concentration)
- Multi-country collaboration: 3.8% → 26.4% (7× increase)

**Speaking points:**
- "Two key metrics demonstrate true globalization"
- "The HHI—a measure of geographic concentration—dropped by 50%, indicating trials are more evenly distributed globally"
- "International collaboration increased 7-fold, with over a quarter of recent trials involving multiple countries"

---

### SLIDE 6: Regional Shift (1 minute)
**Figure:** `slide_regional_shift.png`

**Key numbers:**
| Region | 2001 | 2025 | Change |
|--------|------|------|--------|
| North America | 34.6% | 19.5% | ↓ |
| Europe | 38.5% | 55.5% | ↑ |
| Asia | 26.9% | 52.7% | ↑ |

**Speaking points:**
- "The regional composition has shifted dramatically"
- "North America's share decreased from 35% to under 20%"
- "Europe and Asia now each contribute over half of all trials"
- "This represents a fundamental redistribution of clinical trial activity"

---

### SLIDE 7: Country Leadership (1 minute)
**Figure:** `slide_country_leaders.png`

**Key table:**
| Rank | Overall (2001-2025) | Last 5 Years |
|------|---------------------|--------------|
| 1 | United States (676) | **China (238)** |
| 2 | China (322) | United States (201) |
| 3 | United Kingdom (293) | United Kingdom (141) |

**Speaking points:**
- "The United States remains the overall leader with 676 trials"
- "However, **China has emerged as the top contributor in the last 5 years**, surpassing the US"
- "This leadership transition highlights the rapidly expanding research capacity in Asia"

### SLIDE 8: Comparator Analysis (30 seconds)

**Speaking points:**
- "In summary, our five key findings..."

### SLIDE 10: Conclusions & Implications (45 seconds)
**Content:**
1. Robotic surgery clinical trials have expanded dramatically (11× in 25 years)
2. True globalization: decreased concentration, increased collaboration
3. Regional shift: Europe and Asia now lead
4. China has emerged as the top contributor in recent years
5. **Implication:** The evidence base for robotic surgery is now truly global

**Speaking points:**
- "In conclusion, robotic surgery clinical trials have expanded and globalized substantially"
- "The evidence base is no longer concentrated in a few countries"
- "Policy makers and clinicians should recognize this global distribution when synthesizing evidence"
- "Future work could examine quality metrics and outcomes across regions"

## Presentation Tips

1. **Practice to 7:30** - Leave buffer for transitions and nerves
2. **Lead with the story** - This is about globalization, not just numbers
3. **Emphasize the leadership shift** - China overtaking US is the headline finding
4. **Use animations sparingly** - Build complex figures if needed
5. **Prepare for questions:**
   - "Why did you use journal country as fallback?" 
   - "How did you handle multi-author papers?"
   - "What specialties are driving the growth?"
   - "What about trial quality differences across regions?"
